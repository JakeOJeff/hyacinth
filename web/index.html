Ask Hyacinth

AI · grounded in the data

Ask in English or Malayalam. It answers from today's forecast and the satellite record since 2025, and for "what if" questions it re-runs the simulation on your GPU.

  • Clearance crews Where mats will lock in first, so harvesters go there before it jams.
  • Boats, ferries, houseboats Which stretches to avoid tomorrow and around what time.

  • Officials and researchers Lake health, seasons, algae, mud and debris hotspots since 2025.
